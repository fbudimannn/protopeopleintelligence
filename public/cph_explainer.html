STEP 1 The Standard SHRM Cost-per-Hire (CPH) Formula

How organizations mathematically measure direct recruitment expenditures.

Official SHRM / ANSI Standard Formula
Cost per Hire (CPH) = [ Σ(External Costs) + Σ(Internal Costs) ] ÷ Total Number of Hires

📦 1. External Recruitment Costs

  • Job Portal Subscriptions: Glints, Jobstreet, LinkedIn Recruiter seat costs.
  • Digital Advertising: Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook) and TikTok recruitment campaigns.
  • Recruitment Agencies: Success fees paid to third-party headhunters (typically 1–1.5x monthly gross salary).
  • Background & Health Checks: Pre-employment medical screenings and certifications.

⏱️ 2. Internal Recruitment Costs

  • Recruiter Labor Time: Hourly rate of Talent Acquisition specialists × hours spent sourcing, screening, and scheduling.
  • Hiring Manager Time: Club General Manager / Area Manager hours spent conducting final interviews.
  • ATS & Software Licenses: Applicant Tracking Systems, talent pooling databases, assessment tools.
  • Employee Referral Payouts: Incentives given to current gym staff for successful referrals.

👥 3. Total Hires (Denominator)

  • The total headcount who actually signed offers and completed Day 1 onboarding.
  • Crucial Flaw: The standard formula stops here! It does not account for whether the hire stays 1 month or 1 year, nor does it account for lost revenue while the gym floor was understaffed!
Real-World FIT HUB Frontline Example (Quarterly Hiring Run):
Suppose FIT HUB needs to hire 50 Personal Trainers in a quarter:
• Total External Costs (Job Portal slots + Meta Ads + Agency placements) = Rp 65.000.000
• Total Internal Costs (TA team screening hours + GM interview time + referral bonuses) = Rp 45.000.000
• Standard CPH: (Rp 65.000.000 + Rp 45.000.000) ÷ 50 = Rp 2.200.000 per hire.

STEP 3 The Second Hidden Cost: The 68% Replacement Trap (Early Churn < 6 Months)

What is the financial cost when a contract hire quits within 90 to 180 days?

In fitness retail, 68% of staff attrition occurs in the first 6 months (often before contract completion). When a hire leaves at Month 3, the company doesn't just lose their salary; all onboarding, certification, and training investments are completely wiped out.

Loss Component 1
Rp 2.200.000
Sunk Initial Hiring Spend

The original cost to advertise, screen, interview, and process the candidate is 100% lost.

Loss Component 2
Rp 3.500.000
Onboarding & Certification

Uniforms, gym orientation, FIT HUB Academy modules, and GM 1-on-1 coaching time are gone.

Loss Component 3
Rp 4.200.000
Ramp-Up Productivity Deficit

In Months 1–2, the employee operated at ~50% capacity while earning full pay. They quit just as they reached full productivity.

Loss Component 4
Rp 7.600.000
Re-Hiring & Vacancy Cycle

HR must now re-advertise, re-interview, and leave the position vacant for another 30–40 days.

The Replacement Trap Financial Impact
≈ Rp 17.500.000 Total Destruction per Churned Employee
Equal to ~35% - 40% of the employee's annualized base compensation.
